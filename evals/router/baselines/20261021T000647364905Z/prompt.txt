Choose exactly one intent by the requested task, in the priority order below; do not answer. Incidental tax, retirement, deduction, or account mentions do not determine intent.

When a request includes UI scope or formatting instructions followed by 'Question:', classify the actual question; exchange restrictions and quote-format hints alone do not make an education question market or news.

1. tax: IRS/tax education, treatment, filing status, deductions, credits, planning, or retirement-account rules (Roth/Traditional/SEP/SIMPLE IRA, 401(k), 403(b), similar plans), including eligibility, contributions/limits, withdrawals/distributions, conversions, definitions, and comparisons of those rules.

2. finance_qa: General investing concepts, securities, funds/ETFs, benchmarks, options mechanics, compounding explanations and single-principal simple/compound-interest calculations, diversification, and risk; tax/retirement background alone does not exclude it.

General causal explanations of interest rates, inflation, discount rates, or their effects on stocks/sectors are finance_qa, unless the user explicitly requests recent events, news, or a dated market move. Mentioning technology stocks does not make an educational question news. 'How can higher interest rates affect technology stocks?' -> finance_qa. 'What recent news links higher interest rates to technology stocks?' -> news.

3. portfolio: Analysis of an individual's holdings or a portfolio manager's customer portfolios: value, allocation, concentration, diversification, unrealized performance, screening, comparisons, or contextual alignment with a stored investment profile. Portfolio-specific requests belong here even when they mention investing concepts or a company name. Missing customer/manager identity or portfolio scope stays in portfolio; the portfolio agent asks for clarification rather than guessing.
'Analyze my portfolio.' -> portfolio; 'Show my portfolio allocation.' -> portfolio; 'How is my portfolio performing?' -> portfolio; 'Which of my customers have concentrated portfolios?' -> portfolio; 'What percentage of my portfolio is Apple?' -> portfolio. 'What is Apple's current stock price?' -> market; 'What is an ETF?' -> finance_qa; 'What is the latest Apple news?' -> news.

4. market: Current prices, quotes, volume, and daily change for stocks, ETFs (including AAPL and QQQ), forex, or crypto; company names and missing or ambiguous symbols are resolved by the market agent. Historical prices and time-series windows, including weekly/daily data and calendar or fiscal quarters, also route to market; that agent owns timeframe clarification. Single-asset price growth, returns, or percentage changes over a horizon and cross-asset price-performance comparisons also route to market.

5. goals: Personal savings targets and planning with regular monthly contributions, including projected savings, contributions needed, time to reach a target, and whether a savings plan can meet a deadline. Route all such requests to goals; the goal agent chooses its own tools. Also covers savings targets, future values, or planning calculations, except tax-advantaged account rules and single-principal simple/compound-interest calculations (which belong to finance_qa).

6. news: Current AI, tech, market, or industry news summaries, breaking news, recent earnings announcements, and macroeconomic or market-narrative headlines. Latest Apple news or news affecting semiconductor stocks belongs to news; AAPL current prices or historical price data belongs to market. Identifiable news requests route to news even if scope needs clarification.

7. clarify: Domain unclear (e.g. 'Which is better?'). Identifiable tax/finance_qa/portfolio/market/goals/news requests stay in that domain despite missing income, filing status, year, comparands, target, deadline, contribution, or other details; their respective agents will handle clarification.

Examples: 'What will be my tax bracket?' -> tax; 'Compare two ETFs' -> finance_qa; 'Why did the market drop today after earnings?' -> news.

8. other: Outside these categories.
